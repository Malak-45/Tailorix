✨ Tailorix — Design It. Wear It. Make It Yours. 👗🎀

What if you could design your clothes exactly the way you imagine them?

Tailorix is a custom clothing platform that brings the idea of online tailoring to life. Instead of simply choosing a ready-made outfit, customers can create their own piece from scratch and personalize every detail.

🎀 Choose your style — Girly, Youth, Kids, Casual, Formal, or Sporty.
👗 Choose the clothing type, size, fit, and measurements.
🎨 Pick your favorite color, fabric, pattern, print, or embroidery.
✨ Add special details like pockets, zippers, buttons, sleeves, or a hood.
💰 Get a dynamic price based on your unique design.
🛍️ Add multiple custom pieces to your order.
📦 Track your order from Design Confirmed to Being Tailored and finally Delivered.

The system also includes custom measurements, promo codes, delivery options, payment methods, order management, invoices, and store statistics to create a complete digital tailoring experience.

💻 Built with C++ fundamentals — Arrays, Functions, Loops, Conditions, Strings, Calculations, and Data Management — without using OOP.

🌷 Tailorix isn't just about buying clothes… it's about creating something that feels like YOU.
